# Task 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Insights
## Automotive Market Analytics: Car Sales, Pricing Trends & Performance Insights

## 1. Price Distribution
Most cars are priced between ₹4–12 lakhs, indicating a strong mid-range market.
A small number of luxury vehicles create a right-skewed price distribution.

## 2. Brand Distribution
Hyundai, Maruti, Tata, and Toyota dominate the dataset, showing high market presence.
Premium brands appear less frequently but contribute to higher price values.

## 3. Fuel Type Analysis
Petrol cars are more common, while diesel cars generally have higher prices.
This reflects consumer preference for petrol vehicles and performance demand for diesel cars.

## 4. Transmission Type
Manual cars are more frequent than automatic cars.
Automatic vehicles are priced higher on average, indicating premium positioning.

## 5. Car Age vs Price
There is a clear negative relationship between car age and price.
Newer cars (0–5 years) retain significantly higher value.

## 6. Engine Capacity vs Price
Higher engine capacity is strongly associated with higher car prices.
Vehicles above 2000cc fall mainly into the premium segment.

## 7. Fuel Type vs Price
Diesel cars show a higher median price compared to petrol cars.
This may be due to better torque and long-distance efficiency.

## 8. Correlation Analysis
Strong positive correlation exists between:
- engine_cc and car_price
- max_power_bhp and car_price

Strong negative correlation exists between:
- car_age and car_price

## 9. Key Takeaways
Car pricing is heavily influenced by engine capacity, power, and age.
Popular brands dominate the market, while premium brands drive high-end pricing.
The dataset is now well-prepared for dashboard creation and storytelling.

## 10. Conclusion
This EDA uncovered meaningful trends in pricing, performance, and market behavior.
The insights serve as a strong foundation for Task 3 — Dashboard Creation.
