# Task 3 — Dashboard & Storytelling

## Objective
To visualize automotive market data and identify key factors affecting car pricing.

## Key Insights
1. Car prices decrease significantly as car age increases.
2. Higher engine capacity strongly influences car pricing.
3. Diesel cars are priced higher than petrol cars on average.
4. Automatic transmission vehicles are positioned as premium.
5. Pricing varies across different locations.

## Business Value
The dashboard helps stakeholders understand pricing trends,
identify high-value segments, analyze depreciation,
and make informed inventory and pricing decisions.

## Conclusion
This interactive dashboard converts analytical insights into business-ready visuals.
