# Task 4 — Key Insights

## 1. Pricing vs Car Age
Car prices decrease significantly as car age increases, indicating strong depreciation in the used car market.

## 2. Engine Capacity Impact
Cars with higher engine capacity tend to have higher prices, showing performance as a major pricing driver.

## 3. Fuel Type Trends
Diesel vehicles are priced higher on average compared to petrol vehicles, likely due to fuel efficiency and demand.

## 4. Transmission Preferences
Automatic transmission cars are positioned as premium offerings, with higher average prices than manual cars.

## 5. Brand-Based Pricing
Premium brands consistently command higher prices, while mass-market brands dominate volume sales.

## 6. Location-Based Pricing
Car prices vary across locations, indicating regional demand and purchasing power differences.
